## Why this file and not another

Three independent tests ride on one download, and the first one can **refute a mechanism we
have already published**.

### 1. THE HEADLINE: does the union-tax explanation survive a model with no experts?

Prereg #71 measured MTP on the **MoE split**: K=2 paid **1.114x** (93.2% acceptance — the
first positive MoE-split speculation this box ever measured), and **K=4 collapsed to 0.61x**.
We published the mechanism: the K+1-position verify batch activates the **union of routed
experts** on the CPU-resident path, and no acceptance rate pays that union back. That
explanation is now load-bearing — it scopes D-09, it qualifies the speculation map's MoE cell,
and it is quoted as physics rather than as a fit.

**A dense 9B has no experts to union.** Every verify position re-reads the *same* weights, so
the K+1 batch amortises the read instead of multiplying it. If the union-tax story is right,
the K=4 collapse **must not reproduce here**. If K=4 collapses on a dense model too, our
published mechanism is wrong, and the real cause is something we have not named — the leading
alternative being verify-batch **geometry** (X-1 measured a kernel switch at width 9 on this
card class, and a K=4 verify batch is 5 wide — below that boundary, which is why the alternative
is not yet excluded by anything we own).

This is the cheapest available refutation test of a claim we are currently making in public.

### 2. A published number with a checkable provenance gap

The card claims **GSM8K 95.28%** (four-run average, **vLLM NVFP4**) and **MMLU-Pro Math 92.40%**
(**MTP-Q8_0 GGUF, 8K ctx**). Two different runtimes, two different numeric formats, and the
headline number is *not* the GGUF one. Our EV-1 harness (lm-eval 0.4.12, pinned) runs the same
GSM8K against our own GGUF placement — so the gap between 95.28% and what we measure is
decomposable into quantisation + harness + runtime rather than left as a vibe.

### 3. The capability-table row that tests P-A3 a second time

Phase A found generation beats size (4B x lanes 88.4% > 30B single 87.8%). A 9B math distill
claiming to beat far larger models is the same claim from the other direction. It lands as a
