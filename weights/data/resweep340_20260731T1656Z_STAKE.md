## 0. What is being re-tested and why the old evidence does not carry

Two defects were FOUND by the 340-cell sweep in `weights/exp54_binding_constraint.py` and then
fixed. The sweep has not been re-run since. The artifacts on disk
(`weights/data/exp54_binding_constraint.json`, `weights/data/exp54_p3a_direction.json`) were
written at commit 0782477, and `quantprobe/plan.py` has taken **six** further commits since
(a720457, 54b7dc0-era follow-ons, 03203bc, ac6b8e1, 8db0199, ae4c073, 8c65172). Smoke tests cover
the two MECHANISMS; they do not prove the specific cells now advise correctly.

    D-1  upgrade-advisor defect. The three shipped call sites passed neither `n_layer` nor
         `true_size_gb`, so every counterfactual was evaluated against a SMALLER ROW MENU and a
         DIFFERENT MODEL SIZE than the baseline it was compared to. 31 of 340 cells printed
         different upgrade lines once the inputs matched. The published direction claim
         ("one-directional, can only SUPPRESS") was itself later REFUTED: the bug INVENTED 30
         upgrade recommendations against 3 it suppressed. The arithmetic tell of an invented
         pair is on the record: a RAM-CAPACITY lever and an I/O lever printed at an IDENTICAL
         tok/s (e.g. llama-70b/colibri/ctx16384: `+16 GB RAM` and `NVMe SSD` both x1.70 on a
         128 GB box). Two levers on two different resources cannot honestly buy the same number.

    D-2  speculation-headline defect. The 4.7x ngram headline is arithmetically unreachable on a
         row whose token is largely CPU attention over the KV cache (a verify batch amortizes
         weight READS, not per-position attention). Unreachable on 17 of the 127 cells where
         `run()` actually prints speculation advice.

## 3. Anti-tautology notes (what could make this test unable to fail)

  * The reachable bound in P-4 is recomputed by the auditor from `resource_times(row)` with the
    formula written out, NOT by calling `plan.spec_reachable_x`. Calling the shipped function to
    check the shipped function's own gate would agree by construction.
  * P-1's identical-tok/s test needs no baseline module at all: it is an internal-consistency
    check on the printed page, so it cannot be defeated by a bad baseline ref.
  * P-2 inspects the ACTUAL kwargs of the ACTUAL `evaluate` calls the shipped `run()` makes. It
    does not re-implement the call sites (the old P-3a did, and a re-implementation agrees with
    the reading that produced it).
  * P-6 is the required "construct the input that makes it fail" arm. Its result gates the
    whole verdict via K-C.
  * NOTHING here runs twice on the same target in a way that could measure a warm cache: there
    is no cache, no disk read under test, and no timer.
