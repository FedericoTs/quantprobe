# media/ — the share gallery

Branded, screenshot-ready assets for X/Reddit. Everything here is generated by a script in
`weights/` from **committed measurements** (the cite-or-refuse rule: a generator refuses any
number it cannot source), and everything wears the house brand — the pixel-Q, teal on
`#14181f`, and the tier-gradient bar (VRAM `#e8a87c` → RAM `#d97757` → disk `#a84b32`) from
the wordmark. Regenerate any asset by re-running its generator; never hand-edit an SVG here.

| asset | what it shows | generator | data |
|---|---|---|---|
| [card_flagship.svg](card_flagship.svg) | speed report card: 30B MoE at 20.7 t/s on a 2016 GTX 1060, predicted-vs-measured badge, binding constraint, exact flags | `weights/make_report_card.py` | `weights/data/card_flagship.json` |
| [eval_table.svg](eval_table.svg) | the capability table: model × strategy on one machine; the best cell is a lanes column, not the biggest model | `weights/make_eval_table.py` | `weights/data/grid_*.json` |

predict-then-measure card. Every asset lands here the day its verdict is scored.
